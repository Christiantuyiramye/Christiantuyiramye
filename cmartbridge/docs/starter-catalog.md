# CmartBridge Rwanda — Starter Catalog (20 products)

Phase 1 selection: small, light, cheap, high-demand, legally simple. Nothing with lithium batteries, no food/supplements, no oversize liquids, nothing over ~2 kg.

Door prices are **all-in**: product + shipping + duty + VAT + 30% commission + 5% buffer, rounded up to 500 RWF. They are generated from the same code that powers the website estimator — run `npm run catalog:print` in `cmartbridge/` to regenerate after changing rates.

> ⚠️ Korea prices are retail estimates (Coupang / Olive Young / Dongdaemun) — re-check before quoting. Duty rates are EAC CET placeholders — verify with RRA. Validate each item against Kigali market prices and AliExpress landed cost before launch (see the 90-day plan, weeks 3–4).

## Why these 20

- **Skincare (6):** the K-wave core; Olive Young bestsellers with proven Africa demand and long shelf life.
- **Phone accessories (4):** everyone in Kigali has a phone; genuine Korean-market chargers/cables beat the fakes sold locally. No batteries → no shipping problems.
- **Fashion (4):** Dongdaemun wholesale margin upside; light and unbreakable.
- **K-pop (3):** highest willingness-to-pay per gram; photocards are nearly pure margin on shipping weight.
- **Stationery (3):** cheap entry item — a first order that builds trust for a bigger second order.

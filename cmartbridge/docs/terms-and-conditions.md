## English

### 1. Who we are
CmartBridge Rwanda ("we", "us") is a personal-shopping and delivery service. We buy items from retailers in South Korea on your behalf and deliver them to you in Kigali, Rwanda. We are registered with the Rwanda Development Board (RDB) and act as importer of record for the goods we bring in. *(Registration no.: {RDB_no}, TIN: {TIN}.)*

### 2. The price you pay
- Every quote is **one all-in "delivered to your door" price in RWF**: product cost, international shipping, Rwandan import duty, VAT, our 30% service commission on the product price, and a small buffer for exchange-rate movements.
- **There are no additional charges at delivery. Ever.** If customs charges more than we estimated, that is our cost, not yours.
- A cost breakdown of any quote is available on request.
- Quotes are valid for the number of days stated on the quote (normally 3), because exchange rates and Korean retail prices move.

### 3. Payment
- We accept MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money (preferred), and bank transfer.
- **100% payment is required before we purchase anything.** Your order is confirmed only when payment has cleared.
- We send written (WhatsApp) confirmation of every payment received.

### 4. Delivery time
- Standard delivery is **2–4 weeks** from confirmed payment: we buy in Korea within the weekly buying cycle, consolidate weekly shipments, ship via Korea Post EMS or courier, clear customs in Kigali, then deliver to your address in Kigali or a pickup point.
- You receive WhatsApp updates at five points: payment received → purchased (with a photo of your actual item) → shipped (with tracking number) → arrived in Kigali → cleared / out for delivery.
- Delivery times are estimates. Airline schedules and customs can add delay; if your order is delayed beyond 4 weeks we will tell you why and give a new date.

### 5. Lost or damaged items
- Every shipment is tracked.
- If your item is **lost in transit or arrives damaged**, you choose: we **reship a replacement at no cost** or we **refund 100% of what you paid**.
- Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos.

### 6. If customs holds an item
- We handle all clearance with the Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) at no extra cost to you.
- If an item is permanently refused entry or cannot be released, we refund you in full.

### 7. Refunds & cancellations
- You may cancel free of charge any time **before we purchase** the item in Korea.
- After purchase, items are bought in your name and generally cannot be returned to the Korean retailer; refunds after purchase are limited to the lost / damaged / customs cases above.
- Refunds are paid to the same MoMo/Airtel/bank account that paid us, within 5 working days of the decision.

### 8. What we won't buy
We decline (politely, with a reason) anything with lithium batteries, food, drinks or supplements, liquids/aerosols over airline limits, items over ~2 kg, counterfeit goods, and anything restricted under Korean export law or Rwandan import law. The current checklist is published on our page.

### 9. Your information
We keep your name, phone number and order history to run the service and improve what we stock. We never sell your data. Purchase photos of your items may be shared publicly **only with your permission**.

### 10. Contact & complaints
WhatsApp is our primary channel: {whatsapp_number}. We aim to answer within 24 hours. These terms are governed by the laws of Rwanda.
